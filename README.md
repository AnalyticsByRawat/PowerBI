**Unleashing Sales Analytics Potential**

**📌 Project Overview**

Unleashing Sales Analytics Potential is an interactive Sales & Customer Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes sales transactions across different branches, cities, customer types, genders, product lines, payment methods, and months. It provides a consolidated view of business performance and helps identify sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance.

The project demonstrates the use of Power BI, data visualization, data analysis, Power Query, and DAX to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**🎯 Project Objectives**

The main objectives of this project are to:

Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
Monitor monthly sales trends
Compare sales performance across branches
Analyze sales by gender
Understand customer purchasing behavior
Compare Member vs Normal customers
Identify the top-performing product lines
Analyze sales by payment method
Monitor average customer ratings
Provide interactive filtering for better analysis

**📊 Dashboard Visualizations**

**1. Monthly Sales Trend**

A line chart is used to analyze how revenue changes across different months.

This helps identify:

  Monthly sales patterns
  High-performing months
  Low-performing months
Changes in sales performance over time

**2. Sales by Branch**

A column chart compares sales across the three branches:

  Branch A
  Branch B
  Branch C

This helps identify which branch contributes the most to overall revenue.

**3. Sales by Gender**

The dashboard compares sales generated by:

  Female customers
  Male customers

This provides insight into customer demographics and purchasing behavior.

**4. Top 5 Selling Product Lines**

A bar chart highlights the Top 5 product lines based on sales performance.
The analysis helps identify the product categories contributing significantly to revenue.

**5. Sales by Customer Type**

A donut chart compares sales between:

  Member customers
  Normal customers

This helps understand the contribution of different customer segments.

**6. Payment Method Analysis**

The dashboard analyzes sales generated through:

    Cash
    Ewallet
    Credit Card
